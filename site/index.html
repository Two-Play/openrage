Explore and mod RAGE engine games

OpenRAGE is a free, open-source explorer and modding toolkit for Rockstar's RAGE engine games, on Linux and Windows. Browse and search every archive, preview textures, models, sounds and animations, read metadata as XML, edit game text and install .oiv packages — compatible with OpenIV's mods folder, with undo.

OpenRAGE is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. It contains no game files and no encryption keys: keys are taken from your own copy of the game at runtime.

Highlights

Everything you need to look inside the game and change it safely — from the explorer or the command line.

  • 🗂️Explorer

    Archives — even nested ones — behave like folders. Breadcrumbs, format icons, context menus, keyboard navigation, light and dark mode.

  • 🔎Search everything

    Names, hashes (0xB779A091 → adder) and full text of the whole game in milliseconds, from an index that updates itself.

  • 🖼️Textures

    All formats of GTA V Legacy and Enhanced (incl. BC7); DDS/PNG export identical to OpenIV; replace, add and remove.

  • 🚗Models

    Complete vehicles and peds in a GPU viewer: orbit or fly, show/hide parts, paint materials, export glTF for Blender.

  • 🔊Audio

    Plays every sound and radio track of the game, with waveform, seeking and WAV export.

  • 🏃Animations

    Clip dictionaries play on skinned models; fitting models are suggested from the bones a clip moves.

  • 📄Metadata

    .ymap, .ytyp, .ymt, .ymf, .cut and .pso as XML (RSC meta, PSO and RBF), compatible with CodeWalker.

  • 🎬Cutscenes

    Play a whole cutscene: every actor animated in one scene, through the cutscene camera or a free camera.

  • 🧱Collisions, paths, nav meshes

    .ybn, .ynd, .ynv, .yvr and .ywr in 3D, coloured by material or kind.

  • 📜Scripts and particles

    Disassembles compiled scripts (.ysc); lists effects, textures and models of particle dictionaries (.ypt).

  • 🌐Game text

    Edit .gxt2 text tables in OpenIV's .oxt form and save them back losslessly.

  • 🧩Mods, safely

    Changes go to OpenIV's mods folder, never into your game files; every change and every .oiv package can be undone.
